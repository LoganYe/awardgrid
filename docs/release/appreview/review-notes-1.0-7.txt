AWARDGRID 1.0 (7): BUSINESS MODEL AND GUIDELINE 3.1.3(f)
AwardGrid is a free, independent iPhone companion to the third-party award-search tool seats.aero. We ask App Review to evaluate it under Guideline 3.1.3(f), Free Stand-alone Apps. AwardGrid has no paid tier, In-App Purchases, subscriptions, checkout, purchase links or calls to purchase outside the app. The developer does not sell or issue seats.aero access and receives no payment or commission from seats.aero. An existing API-enabled seats.aero account supplies the user's own data; it does not unlock a developer-sold AwardGrid tier.

CHANGES SINCE 1.0 (3), REJECTED UNDER GUIDELINE 3.1.1 ON 5 OCTOBER 2026
- There is no field for entering a key of any kind. The optional connection uses only Login with Seats.aero (OAuth 2.0), the provider's own sign-in and consent page.
- Search, List, Calendar, Matrix, option details, Compare, Saved and Watches work with built-in, clearly labelled sample data without an account. Sample data has no booking links.
- The app has no purchase prompts or "Pro" wording. Ask, the optional AI feature that used an Anthropic API key, is absent from this App Store build.

BUILD 7 DATA HANDLING
Account results are cached only in memory, not written to app files. Saved keeps query conditions; after restart, reopening searches again. Watches keep conditions and rebuild their baseline. Cleanup errors for older stored data are reported instead of claiming complete deletion.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
1. On Welcome, tap "Try with sample data".
2. Search a route with a date window, for example "SFO to NRT next 60 days business".
3. Switch between List, Calendar and Matrix, change filters, and open option details.
4. Select two options and tap "Compare selected options". Tap "Save option" to use Saved and "Watch this search" to use Watches.
5. Tap "Exit sample data" in the banner to return to Welcome.
The screens show "Sample data" and "Illustrative data — not live availability"; sample results are labelled "Sample data". The app generates them on the device for routes it recognises.
We request acceptance of this built-in sample-data mode as the review path under Guideline 2.1. We asked about this in our 6 October message; Apple has not yet given written approval of this review path.

OPTIONAL CONNECTION AND REVIEW ACCESS
After exiting sample data, tap "Connect your seats.aero account" on Welcome, or open Settings > seats.aero account, then "Connect seats.aero". The system sign-in sheet opens seats.aero's own website. The user signs in and authorises AwardGrid there; AwardGrid never sees the password. Only an existing account with API access can supply account results.
seats.aero confirmed in writing on 7 October 2026 that AwardGrid should use Login with Seats.aero, and on 8 October that its terms allow this connection for commercial use. Its registration of AwardGrid is still unapproved; the provider's consent page currently warns that the app has not been reviewed by seats.aero. We will release manually after that registration is approved.
seats.aero's account terms prohibit sharing accounts, so we cannot provide the owner's credentials or a shared account for review. Sample data gives account-free access to the app's search and organisation features. No recording is currently attached to App Review Information.
Connected results show the required "Data: seats.aero" attribution. Searches go directly from the device to seats.aero. The token service at awardgrid.dowhiz.com only exchanges and renews OAuth tokens and stores nothing; tokens stay in the iOS Keychain.

AwardGrid has no accounts of its own, analytics, ads or tracking. Please contact the App Review contact on file (Pacific Time, English).
