Support

AwardGrid for iPhone

Write to %AWARDGRID_SUPPORT_EMAIL%. Say what you tapped, what you expected and what happened. Never send an API key: the app shows only a key's last four characters, and that is all anyone helping you needs.

What it needs

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
  • Your own seats.aero Pro subscription and its API key, from the API tab of your seats.aero settings. Without it the app searches nothing.
  • For Ask, which is optional: your own Anthropic API key. Anthropic bills each question to that key, and the app asks your permission before it sends anything to Anthropic.

Common questions

Why does a search use seats.aero calls?

Each search goes to seats.aero with your key, and seats.aero counts calls against your plan's daily limit. The app stops at 950 of the 1,000 a Pro key allows, and keeps results on the device for 45 minutes, so repeating a search costs nothing. The count starts again at midnight UTC.

What does "not monitored" mean?

seats.aero does not track that route for the programs you asked about, so no result there is not the same as no seats. A route whose list could not be loaded says so instead.

When are watches checked?

When you open the app or come back to it, and at no other time. iOS cannot promise to run an app while you are not using it, so AwardGrid does not claim to.

Are the results live?

They are seats.aero's cached availability, with how old each result is. Awards disappear; confirm on the program's own site before you transfer any points.

How do I remove my keys or my data?

Remove either key in Settings, clear cached results there, and start a new conversation in Ask to delete the conversation. The privacy policy lists everything the app keeps and sends.

常见问题

为什么查票会消耗 seats.aero 调用？

每次查票都会携带你的密钥发往 seats.aero，seats.aero 按你套餐的每日上限计数。Pro 密钥每天允许 1,000 次，应用在 950 次时停止；结果在本机保留 45 分钟，重复查询不消耗调用。计数在 UTC 午夜重新开始。

“未监控”是什么意思？

seats.aero 没有为你所查的里程计划跟踪这条航线，所以那里没有结果不等于没有座位。航线列表无法加载时，应用会如实说明。

关注什么时候检查？

在你打开或回到应用时检查，其他时间不会。iOS 无法保证应用在你没有打开它时运行，所以 AwardGrid 不作这样的承诺。

结果是实时的吗？

结果是 seats.aero 的缓存数据，并注明每条结果的数据时间。里程票随时会消失；转点前请先在里程计划官网确认。

如何移除密钥或数据？

在设置中移除任一密钥、清除缓存结果；在 AI 辅助中开始新对话即可删除对话。隐私政策列出了应用保存和发送的全部内容。
