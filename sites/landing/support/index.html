AwardGrid for iPhone

Write to %AWARDGRID_SUPPORT_EMAIL%. Say what you tapped, what you expected and what happened. Never send your seats.aero password: AwardGrid never sees it, and nobody helping you needs it.

What it needs

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
  • Optional: your own seats.aero account with API access (part of seats.aero Pro, which AwardGrid does not sell), connected with seats.aero's own sign-in. Without it, AwardGrid shows sample data only.

Common questions

Do I need a seats.aero account?

No. Without one, AwardGrid shows sample data only: search, the List, Calendar and Matrix views, option details, compare, Saved and Watches all work on sample data made on your iPhone, labelled as sample data, and nothing is sent. If you have a seats.aero account with API access, you can connect it in Settings to see results from that account instead.

How do I connect my seats.aero account?

In AwardGrid, open Settings › seats.aero account and tap Connect seats.aero. It opens seats.aero's own sign-in (Login with Seats.aero), where you sign in to seats.aero and approve AwardGrid. AwardGrid never sees your password. You can disconnect at any time, in AwardGrid or in your seats.aero settings.

My seats.aero account has no API access. Will AwardGrid work?

Only on sample data. seats.aero says not every Pro account or country gets API access; without it, AwardGrid shows sample data only. AwardGrid depends on seats.aero's Partner API and connects to your seats.aero account through seats.aero's own sign-in (Login with Seats.aero); seats.aero's terms apply to that account.

Why does a search use seats.aero calls?

Each search on your seats.aero account goes from the iPhone directly to seats.aero, and seats.aero counts the calls against a daily limit. The app stops at 950 of seats.aero's 1,000 daily calls and keeps results on the device for 45 minutes, so repeating a search costs no calls. The app's own count restarts at midnight UTC, when seats.aero resets usage. Sample data uses no seats.aero calls.

How long are results from my seats.aero account kept?

Results from your seats.aero account are kept on the iPhone for at most 24 hours (seats.aero's Short-Term Caching rule); a saved search keeps the search and a summary, and opening it searches again.

What does an empty result mean?

An empty result means seats.aero has no cached availability for that route and day, which can also mean seats.aero does not monitor the route. Results are seats.aero's cached availability, not a live search, so no result there is not the same as no seats.

When are watches checked?

When you open the app or come back to it, and at no other time. iOS cannot promise to run an app while you are not using it, so AwardGrid does not claim to.

Are the results live?

They are seats.aero's cached availability, with how old each result is. Awards disappear; confirm on the program's own site before you transfer any points.

How do I disconnect, or remove my data?

Disconnect in Settings › seats.aero account: it removes the sign-in tokens and all results from seats.aero from the iPhone. You can also remove AwardGrid's access in your seats.aero settings. Clear cached results in Settings. Exit sample data to delete everything sample data kept. The privacy policy lists everything the app keeps and sends.

常见问题

需要 seats.aero 账户吗？

不需要。不连接 seats.aero 账户时，AwardGrid 只显示示例数据：查票、列表、日历和矩阵视图、选项详情、比较、收藏和关注都可以使用本机生成的示例数据，并标注为示例数据，不会发送任何内容。如果你有带 API 权限的 seats.aero 账户，可以在设置中连接它，看到该账户的结果。

如何连接我的 seats.aero 账户？

在 AwardGrid 中打开“设置 › seats.aero 账户”，点按“连接 seats.aero”。它会打开 seats.aero 自己的登录页面（Login with Seats.aero），你在那里登录 seats.aero 并批准 AwardGrid。AwardGrid 不会看到你的密码。你可以随时在 AwardGrid 或 seats.aero 设置中断开连接。

我的 seats.aero 账户没有 API 权限，AwardGrid 还能用吗？

只能用示例数据。seats.aero 说明，并非每个 Pro 账户、每个国家或地区都能获得 API 权限；没有 API 权限，AwardGrid 只显示示例数据。AwardGrid 依赖 seats.aero 的 Partner API，并通过 seats.aero 自己的登录（Login with Seats.aero）连接你的 seats.aero 账户；该账户适用 seats.aero 的条款。

为什么查票会消耗 seats.aero 调用？

用你的 seats.aero 账户查票时，每次查票都从 iPhone 直接发往 seats.aero，seats.aero 按每日上限计数。seats.aero 每天允许 1,000 次调用，应用在 950 次时停止；结果在本机保留 45 分钟，重复查询不消耗调用。应用自己的计数在 UTC 午夜重新开始，seats.aero 也在这时重置用量。示例数据不消耗 seats.aero 调用。

来自我的 seats.aero 账户的结果会保留多久？

来自你的 seats.aero 账户的结果在 iPhone 上最多保留 24 小时（seats.aero 的短期缓存规则）；收藏的查询会保留查询条件和摘要，打开时会重新查询。

结果为空是什么意思？

结果为空，表示 seats.aero 没有这条航线在那一天的缓存数据，也可能是 seats.aero 没有监测这条航线。结果来自 seats.aero 的缓存，并非实时查询，所以那里没有结果不等于没有座位。

关注什么时候检查？

在你打开或回到应用时检查，其他时间不会。iOS 无法保证应用在你没有打开它时运行，所以 AwardGrid 不作这样的承诺。

结果是实时的吗？

结果是 seats.aero 的缓存数据，并注明每条结果的数据时间。里程票随时会消失；转点前请先在里程计划官网确认。

如何断开连接或删除数据？

在“设置 › seats.aero 账户”中断开连接：这会从 iPhone 上移除登录令牌和所有来自 seats.aero 的结果。你也可以在 seats.aero 设置中移除 AwardGrid 的访问权限。在设置中清除缓存结果。退出示例数据即可删除示例数据保存的全部内容。隐私政策列出了应用保存和发送的全部内容。
