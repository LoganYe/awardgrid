Award search

One table. Your own key.

Type the routes and dates in English or Chinese, or set them in the editor. It puts seats.aero's cached award availability for several origins, several destinations and up to 92 days into one table. Each cell shows the lowest miles for each cabin you asked for, with the program and the seats left; each option also shows its fees and how old the data is.

No account

There is no signup, no login and no server of ours. The app runs on your device and keeps its results there.

Your own keys

Every search runs on your own seats.aero Pro key, and Ask, if you use it, on your own Anthropic key, both stored in your device's Keychain. There is no shared key and no server key.

Bilingual

Ask in Chinese or English. The table, the dates and the program names read the same in both.

What it does not do

No airline sites It reads the seats.aero cache. If you add your own Anthropic key, Ask also sends your question and the results it reads to Anthropic. It never opens an airline site for you, and it never asks for an airline or bank password.
No guaranteed schedule iOS cannot promise to run anything on a cadence, so AwardGrid does not claim one. It checks your watches when you open the app, and at no other time. There is no background check.
No key of its own Without your seats.aero Pro key the app does nothing at all. It cannot search on someone else's behalf, and nobody can search on yours.
No live search Only seats.aero's cached availability. Cached data goes stale and awards disappear; confirm on the program's own site before transferring points.
No accounts, no analytics in the app No telemetry, no advertising, no tracking. There is no server to collect any of it. These web pages count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies (privacy policy).
