Privacy policy

AwardGrid for iPhone

Last updated 8 October 2026.

In short

  • AwardGrid for iPhone has no accounts of its own and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. Its developer keeps none of your data.
  • With sample data, the app sends nothing: sample data is made on your device.
  • You connect your seats.aero account with seats.aero's own sign-in (Login with Seats.aero): seats.aero asks you to sign in and approve AwardGrid, and AwardGrid never sees your password. You can disconnect at any time, in AwardGrid or in your seats.aero settings.
  • With your account connected, the app sends your search from your device directly to seats.aero, with the access token from that sign-in.
  • A small token service at awardgrid.dowhiz.com exchanges and refreshes the sign-in tokens for AwardGrid; it stores nothing and keeps no logs of tokens.
  • Results from your seats.aero account are kept on the iPhone for at most 24 hours (seats.aero's Short-Term Caching rule); a saved search keeps the search and a summary, and opening it searches again.
  • The App Store version sends nothing to Anthropic: Ask, which does, is only in the app's test builds and in the separate web app.
  • The sign-in tokens, if you connect your account, stay in your device's Keychain. Everything else the app keeps stays in its own storage on your device.
  • This website, not the app, counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies (see This website).

What the app sends, and to whom

Sample data: nothing

Without a seats.aero account, AwardGrid shows sample data: invented results, made on your device for the routes and dates you search, and labelled "Sample data" on every screen that shows them. While sample data is on, searching, flight details, watches and the app's other features send nothing to seats.aero or to anyone else.

seats.aero's sign-in, when you connect

Connect seats.aero (Settings › seats.aero account) opens seats.aero's own sign-in page, Login with Seats.aero, in the system's sign-in sheet. You sign in to seats.aero there and approve AwardGrid; AwardGrid never sees your password. The page receives AwardGrid's client ID and a random value that ties seats.aero's answer to this sign-in. seats.aero then hands a one-time code back to the app, through AwardGrid's token service.

AwardGrid's token service, to exchange and refresh the sign-in tokens

AwardGrid's client secret may not be in the app, so a small token service at awardgrid.dowhiz.com, which runs on Cloudflare Workers, does two things for it. When you connect, it exchanges the one-time code for the sign-in tokens (an access token, valid about an hour, and a refresh token). Later, it gets a new access token with the refresh token. The app sends it only the code and the random value, or the refresh token; the service adds AwardGrid's client ID and secret, passes the request to seats.aero, and hands back only the tokens. It stores nothing and keeps no logs of tokens. It never sees your searches or their results, and, like any server, it sees your device's IP address.

seats.aero, for every search on your account

With your own seats.aero account connected, AwardGrid gets award availability only from the seats.aero Partner API, directly from your device. It sends seats.aero:

  • the access token from your sign-in, in the header that authorizes each request;
  • your search: airports, dates, cabins, programs and similar filters;
  • when you open a result's flight details, that result's availability ID.

These requests are made when you search, when you open flight details, when you open a saved search again after its results were removed, and when you open or return to the app while you have watches (in a test build with Ask, also when Ask searches for a question). seats.aero handles them under its own terms (seats.aero/terms) and privacy practices.

Anthropic: only for Ask, in test builds

The App Store version of AwardGrid for iPhone has no Ask and sends nothing to Anthropic. Ask, which answers questions about your results with Claude on your own Anthropic API key, is in the app's test builds and in the separate web app only. The rest of this section is for a test build of the iPhone app.

In a test build, Ask is optional. It works only with your own Anthropic API key, and before your first question the app asks for your permission to send data to Anthropic. A question sends Anthropic:

  • the question, and today's date;
  • the earlier questions and answers in the same conversation;
  • the search you choose to include, and any results you attach;
  • the seats.aero results of the searches and flight lookups Ask makes for the question, with how many seats.aero calls are left.

Each request also carries your Anthropic API key, headers that name Anthropic's software library and its version, and the Accept-Language header, which gives the app's language and region. Checking your Anthropic key sends a request with no question in it. Your seats.aero credentials are never sent to Anthropic. Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), and bills your account for each question.

You can withdraw the permission on the Anthropic key page in Settings. That stops later questions; it cannot recall what was already sent.

Nothing else

Like any internet request, a request to seats.aero or to the token service (or, in a test build with Ask, to Anthropic) shows that service your device's IP address. The app sends nothing to any other server, and the token service, which the developer runs, keeps nothing it receives. If you choose, in iOS, to share analytics with app developers, or you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports or the feedback you send to the developer; those are Apple's features, under your device's settings and Apple's policies.

What stays on your device

  • Keychain: the sign-in tokens for your seats.aero account, if you connect it (and, in a test build with Ask, your Anthropic key, if you add one). They are kept for this device only and not synced through iCloud Keychain. The app never shows the tokens, and shows only the last four characters of a key.
  • The app's own files: whether you use sample data or your seats.aero account; search results it has cached (for 45 minutes, so repeating a search costs no calls); today's count of seats.aero calls; your watches; the search and results on screen, and how you view them; results you save; trip plans you save; and your settings (language and appearance). In a test build with Ask, also whether Ask may send data to Anthropic, and your Ask conversation, until you start a new one.
  • At most 24 hours: results from seats.aero are kept on the device for at most 24 hours (seats.aero's Short-Term Caching rule). The app removes older ones when it opens, when you return to it and while it stays open: cached results, the results on screen, the results in saved searches, and what your watches last saw. A saved search keeps the search and a summary (when it was saved and how many options it showed), and opening it searches again; your watches keep their conditions.
  • Sample data: while it is on, the app keeps its own copies of these files (its cached results, watches, saved results and plans, the search on screen and settings) in a separate folder, and exiting sample data deletes them. Sample data never reads or changes your sign-in tokens or today's count of seats.aero calls.

The app's files are included in your device's backups, to iCloud or to a computer, when you back up the device.

Your choices

  • Disconnect in Settings › seats.aero account: it removes the sign-in tokens and all results from seats.aero from your device. You can also remove AwardGrid's access in your seats.aero settings; the app then removes them the next time it renews the connection.
  • Clear cached results in Settings.
  • Exit sample data to delete everything sample data kept.
  • In a test build with Ask: remove the Anthropic key in Settings, start a new conversation in Ask to delete the conversation from the device, and withdraw Ask's permission on the Anthropic key page in Settings.
  • Delete the app to delete its files. iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted, so disconnect in Settings first if you want the sign-in tokens gone.

What seats.aero (or, for Ask in a test build, Anthropic) received is held by them, under their own policies; ask them about it. AwardGrid has no copy, so it cannot delete it for you.

The App Store privacy label

The label of the App Store version lists Search History as data linked to you, used for app functionality, and no data used to track you. That is what the app sends to seats.aero on your own account, as set out above. The developer does not receive it.

Tracking and advertising

AwardGrid does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and shows no ads.

This website

The pages on awardgrid.dowhiz.com, this one included, are served through Cloudflare, which counts visits to them with Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script from Cloudflare records the page, the site you came from, your browser, operating system and type of device, your country, and how quickly the page loaded. It uses no cookies and does not fingerprint you, and the developer sees only totals. Like any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address when it serves a page.

The app does not include this script. Its requests to AwardGrid's token service go to Cloudflare, which hosts the service on Cloudflare Workers: the one-time code and the random value when you connect, and the refresh token when the app renews the connection. Opening one of these pages from the app (Settings › Privacy policy or Support) opens it in Safari, as a visit like any other; as you tap the link, iOS may already open a connection to awardgrid.dowhiz.com, which shows Cloudflare your IP address.

Changes

If what the app sends changes, this page changes first, with a new date. In a test build with Ask, if Ask would send something new to Anthropic, the app asks for your permission again.

应用发送什么，发给谁

示例数据：不发送任何内容

没有 seats.aero 账户时，AwardGrid 显示示例数据：在本机按你查询的航线和日期生成的虚构结果，每个显示它的页面都标注“示例数据”。示例数据开启期间，查票、航班详情、关注以及应用的其他功能都不会向 seats.aero 或任何其他方发送内容。

seats.aero 登录：连接账户时

“连接 seats.aero”（设置 › seats.aero 账户）会在系统的登录窗口中打开 seats.aero 自己的登录页面 Login with Seats.aero。你在那里登录 seats.aero 并批准 AwardGrid；AwardGrid 不会看到你的密码。这个页面会收到 AwardGrid 的客户端 ID，以及一个把 seats.aero 的回应与这次登录对应起来的随机值。之后 seats.aero 通过 AwardGrid 的令牌服务，把一个一次性代码交回应用。

AwardGrid 的令牌服务：交换和续期登录令牌

AwardGrid 的客户端密钥不能放在应用里，所以 awardgrid.dowhiz.com 上的一个小型令牌服务（运行在 Cloudflare Workers 上）替应用做两件事：连接时，用一次性代码换取登录令牌（一个约一小时有效的访问令牌和一个刷新令牌）；之后，用刷新令牌换取新的访问令牌。应用只向它发送代码和随机值，或刷新令牌；它加上 AwardGrid 的客户端 ID 和密钥，把请求转给 seats.aero，只把令牌交回。它不保存任何内容，也不记录令牌。它看不到你的查询和查询结果；与任何服务器一样，它会看到你设备的 IP 地址。

seats.aero：用你的账户每次查票

连接你自己的 seats.aero 账户后，AwardGrid 只通过 seats.aero Partner API、从本机直接获取里程票数据。它会向 seats.aero 发送：

  • 这次登录得到的访问令牌，放在为每个请求授权的请求头中；
  • 你的查询：机场、日期、舱位、里程计划及类似筛选条件；
  • 打开某个结果的航班详情时，该结果的 availability ID。

这些请求发生在：你查票时、打开航班详情时、在收藏的结果被移除后再次打开该查询时，以及你有关注并打开或回到应用时（在有 AI 辅助的测试版本中，还包括 AI 辅助为问题查票时）。seats.aero 按其自身的条款（seats.aero/terms）和隐私做法处理这些请求。

Anthropic：仅用于测试版本中的 AI 辅助

AwardGrid iPhone 版的 App Store 版本没有 AI 辅助，不向 Anthropic 发送任何内容。AI 辅助（Ask）用你自己的 Anthropic API 密钥、由 Claude 回答关于结果的问题，只在应用的测试版本和独立的网页应用里。本节其余内容适用于 iPhone 应用的测试版本。

在测试版本中，AI 辅助是可选功能。它只使用你自己的 Anthropic API 密钥；在你第一次提问之前，应用会先征求你同意向 Anthropic 发送数据。一次提问会向 Anthropic 发送：

  • 问题本身和今天的日期；
  • 同一对话中之前的问答；
  • 你选择附带的查询，以及你附上的结果；
  • AI 辅助为这个问题进行的查询和航班查找所得到的 seats.aero 结果，以及剩余的 seats.aero 调用次数。

每个请求还会带上你的 Anthropic API 密钥、标明 Anthropic 软件库及其版本的请求头，以及表示应用语言和地区的 Accept-Language 请求头。检查 Anthropic 密钥时发送的请求不含任何问题。你的 seats.aero 凭据不会发给 Anthropic。Anthropic 按其自身的条款和隐私政策（anthropic.com/legal/privacy）处理这些数据，并按每次提问向你的账户计费。

你可以在设置的 Anthropic 密钥页撤回许可。撤回后不再发送之后的问题；已发送的内容无法撤回。

其他

与任何网络请求一样，发往 seats.aero 或令牌服务（或在有 AI 辅助的测试版本中发往 Anthropic）的请求会让对方看到你设备的 IP 地址。应用不会向任何其他服务器发送数据；由开发者运行的令牌服务不保存它收到的任何内容。如果你在 iOS 中选择与应用开发者共享分析数据，或通过 TestFlight 测试本应用，Apple 可能会把崩溃报告或你提交的反馈转交给开发者；这些是 Apple 的功能，受你的设备设置和 Apple 的政策约束。

留在你设备上的内容

  • 钥匙串：你连接账户后的 seats.aero 登录令牌（在有 AI 辅助的测试版本中，还有你添加的 Anthropic 密钥）。它们只保存在本机，不通过 iCloud 钥匙串同步。应用从不显示令牌，密钥只显示末四位。
  • 应用自己的文件：你使用示例数据还是 seats.aero 账户；缓存的查票结果（保留 45 分钟，重复查询不消耗调用）；今天的 seats.aero 调用次数；你的关注；屏幕上的查询和结果及其显示方式；你保存的结果；你保存的行程规划；以及你的设置（语言和外观）。在有 AI 辅助的测试版本中，还有是否允许 AI 辅助向 Anthropic 发送数据，以及你的 AI 辅助对话，直到你开始新对话。
  • 最多 24 小时：来自 seats.aero 的结果在本机最多保留 24 小时（seats.aero 的短期缓存规则）。应用会在打开时、你回到应用时以及保持打开期间移除更早的结果：缓存结果、屏幕上的结果、收藏中的结果，以及关注上次看到的内容。收藏的查询会保留查询条件和摘要（保存时间和显示过的选项数），打开时会重新查询；关注会保留其条件。
  • 示例数据：开启期间，应用把自己的一套文件（缓存结果、关注、保存的结果和行程规划、屏幕上的查询和设置）放在单独的文件夹里，退出示例数据时会删除它们。示例数据从不读取或更改你的登录令牌，也不读取或更改今天的 seats.aero 调用次数。

你备份设备时（到 iCloud 或电脑），应用的文件会包含在备份中。

你的选择

  • 在“设置 › seats.aero 账户”中断开连接：这会从本机移除登录令牌和所有来自 seats.aero 的结果。你也可以在 seats.aero 设置中移除 AwardGrid 的访问权限；应用下次续期连接时会移除它们。
  • 在设置中清除缓存结果。
  • 退出示例数据，即删除示例数据保存的全部内容。
  • 在有 AI 辅助的测试版本中：在设置中移除 Anthropic 密钥；在 AI 辅助中开始新对话，即从本机删除该对话；在设置的 Anthropic 密钥页撤回 AI 辅助的许可。
  • 删除应用即删除其文件。应用删除后，iOS 可能仍在钥匙串中保留内容；如需删除登录令牌，请先在设置中断开连接。

seats.aero（或在测试版本中为 AI 辅助接收内容的 Anthropic）收到的内容由它们按各自的政策保存，请直接向它们询问。AwardGrid 没有副本，因此无法替你删除。
