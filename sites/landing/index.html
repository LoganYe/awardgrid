AwardGrid for iPhone

Type the routes and dates in English or Chinese, and AwardGrid puts award seats into one table, from your own seats.aero account or from sample data.

Your own seats.aero data (optional): if you have a seats.aero account with API access (part of seats.aero Pro, which AwardGrid does not sell), you can connect it to see results from that account instead of sample data.

AwardGrid for iPhone is submitted to the App Store and not available there yet.
