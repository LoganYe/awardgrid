Privacy policy

AwardGrid for iPhone

Last updated 25 September 2026.

In short

  • AwardGrid has no accounts and no server of its own. Its developer receives none of your data: there is no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting in the app.
  • To search, the app sends your search to seats.aero, with your own seats.aero API key.
  • Only if you add your own Anthropic API key and allow it, Ask sends your questions, and the search data they need, to Anthropic.
  • Your keys stay in your device's Keychain. Everything else the app keeps stays in its own storage on your device.

What the app sends, and to whom

seats.aero, for every search

AwardGrid gets award availability only from the seats.aero Partner API, with the API key you add. It sends seats.aero:

  • your seats.aero API key, in the header that authorizes each request;
  • your search: airports, dates, cabins, programs and similar filters;
  • when you open a result's flight details, that result's availability ID.

These requests are made when you search, when you check a key before saving it, when you open flight details, when you open or return to the app while you have watches, and when Ask searches for a question. seats.aero handles them under its own terms (seats.aero/terms) and privacy practices.

Anthropic, only for Ask, only with your permission

Ask is optional. It works only with your own Anthropic API key, and before your first question the app asks for your permission to send data to Anthropic. A question sends Anthropic:

  • the question, and today's date;
  • the earlier questions and answers in the same conversation;
  • the search you choose to include, and any results you attach;
  • the seats.aero results of the searches and flight lookups Ask makes for the question, with how many seats.aero calls are left.

Each request also carries your Anthropic API key, headers that name Anthropic's software library and its version, and the Accept-Language header, which gives the app's language and region. Checking your Anthropic key sends a request with no question in it. Your seats.aero key is never sent to Anthropic. Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), and bills your account for each question.

You can withdraw the permission on the Anthropic key page in Settings. That stops later questions; it cannot recall what was already sent.

Nothing else

Like any internet request, a request to seats.aero or Anthropic shows that service your device's IP address. The app sends nothing to any other server, and none to its developer. If you choose, in iOS, to share analytics with app developers, or you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may pass crash reports or the feedback you send to the developer; those are Apple's features, under your device's settings and Apple's policies.

What stays on your device

  • Keychain: your seats.aero key and, if you add one, your Anthropic key. They are kept for this device only and not synced through iCloud Keychain. The app shows only their last four characters.
  • The app's own files: search results it has cached (for 45 minutes, so repeating a search costs no calls); today's count of seats.aero calls; your watches; the search and results on screen, and how you view them; results you save; your settings (language, appearance, and whether Ask may send data to Anthropic); and your Ask conversation, until you start a new one.

The app's files are included in your device's backups, to iCloud or to a computer, when you back up the device.

Your choices

  • Remove either key in Settings.
  • Clear cached results in Settings.
  • Start a new conversation in Ask to delete the conversation from the device.
  • Withdraw Ask's permission on the Anthropic key page in Settings.
  • Delete the app to delete its files. iOS may keep the keys in the Keychain after the app is deleted, so remove them in Settings first if you want them gone.

What seats.aero or Anthropic received is held by them, under their own policies; ask them about it. AwardGrid has no copy, so it cannot delete it for you.

The App Store privacy label

The label lists Search History and Other User Content as data linked to you, used for app functionality, and no data used to track you. That is what the app sends to seats.aero and Anthropic on your own accounts, as set out above. The developer does not receive it.

Tracking and advertising

AwardGrid does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and shows no ads.

Changes

If what the app sends changes, this page changes first, with a new date. If Ask would send something new to Anthropic, the app asks for your permission again.

隐私政策

AwardGrid iPhone 版

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 25 日。

概要

  • AwardGrid 没有账号，也没有自己的服务器。开发者不会收到你的任何数据：应用中没有统计分析、广告、跟踪或崩溃报告。
  • 查票时，应用使用你自己的 seats.aero API 密钥，把你的查询发送给 seats.aero。
  • 只有在你添加了自己的 Anthropic API 密钥并允许之后，AI 辅助才会把你的问题及其所需的查询数据发送给 Anthropic。
  • 你的密钥保存在本机钥匙串中。应用保存的其他内容都留在本机上它自己的存储中。

应用发送什么，发给谁

seats.aero：每次查票

AwardGrid 只通过 seats.aero Partner API、使用你添加的 API 密钥获取里程票数据。它会向 seats.aero 发送：

  • 你的 seats.aero API 密钥，放在为每个请求授权的请求头中；
  • 你的查询：机场、日期、舱位、里程计划及类似筛选条件；
  • 打开某个结果的航班详情时，该结果的 availability ID。

这些请求发生在：你查票时、保存前检查密钥时、打开航班详情时、你有关注并打开或回到应用时，以及 AI 辅助为问题查票时。seats.aero 按其自身的条款（seats.aero/terms）和隐私做法处理这些请求。

Anthropic：仅用于 AI 辅助，且需你允许

AI 辅助是可选功能。它只使用你自己的 Anthropic API 密钥；在你第一次提问之前，应用会先征求你同意向 Anthropic 发送数据。一次提问会向 Anthropic 发送：

  • 问题本身和今天的日期；
  • 同一对话中之前的问答；
  • 你选择附带的查询，以及你附上的结果；
  • AI 辅助为这个问题进行的查询和航班查找所得到的 seats.aero 结果，以及剩余的 seats.aero 调用次数。

每个请求还会带上你的 Anthropic API 密钥、标明 Anthropic 软件库及其版本的请求头，以及表示应用语言和地区的 Accept-Language 请求头。检查 Anthropic 密钥时发送的请求不含任何问题。你的 seats.aero 密钥不会发给 Anthropic。Anthropic 按其自身的条款和隐私政策（anthropic.com/legal/privacy）处理这些数据，并按每次提问向你的账户计费。

你可以在设置的 Anthropic 密钥页撤回许可。撤回后不再发送之后的问题；已发送的内容无法撤回。

其他

与任何网络请求一样，发往 seats.aero 或 Anthropic 的请求会让对方看到你设备的 IP 地址。应用不会向任何其他服务器发送数据，也不会发给开发者。如果你在 iOS 中选择与应用开发者共享分析数据，或通过 TestFlight 测试本应用，Apple 可能会把崩溃报告或你提交的反馈转交给开发者；这些是 Apple 的功能，受你的设备设置和 Apple 的政策约束。

留在你设备上的内容

  • 钥匙串：你的 seats.aero 密钥，以及你添加的 Anthropic 密钥。它们只保存在本机，不通过 iCloud 钥匙串同步。应用只显示末四位。
  • 应用自己的文件：缓存的查票结果（保留 45 分钟，重复查询不消耗调用）；今天的 seats.aero 调用次数；你的关注；屏幕上的查询和结果及其显示方式；你保存的结果；你的设置（语言、外观，以及是否允许 AI 辅助向 Anthropic 发送数据）；以及你的 AI 辅助对话，直到你开始新对话。

你备份设备时（到 iCloud 或电脑），应用的文件会包含在备份中。

你的选择

  • 在设置中移除任一密钥。
  • 在设置中清除缓存结果。
  • 在 AI 辅助中开始新对话，即从本机删除该对话。
  • 在设置的 Anthropic 密钥页撤回 AI 辅助的许可。
  • 删除应用即删除其文件。应用删除后，iOS 可能仍在钥匙串中保留密钥；如需删除，请先在设置中移除。

seats.aero 或 Anthropic 收到的内容由它们按各自的政策保存，请直接向它们询问。AwardGrid 没有副本，因此无法替你删除。

App Store 隐私标签

标签列出与你关联的“搜索历史”和“其他用户内容”，用于应用功能，且没有用于跟踪你的数据。这就是上文所述、应用使用你自己的账户发送给 seats.aero 和 Anthropic 的内容。开发者不会收到这些数据。

跟踪与广告

AwardGrid 不会在其他公司的应用和网站之间跟踪你，不使用广告标识符，也不显示广告。

变更

如果应用发送的内容有变，本页会先更新，并注明新的日期。如果 AI 辅助要向 Anthropic 发送新的内容，应用会重新征求你的许可。
